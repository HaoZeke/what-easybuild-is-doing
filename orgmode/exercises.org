Each page includes one or two items, positioned where the page has just taught the concept they test.
This page enumerates all items in reading order.
A taught session may select a subset, and an individual reader can locate the desired item.
Each item possesses an id that remains stable across edits.
The stable id can be referenced aloud, in messages, or in shared documents during a workshop.
