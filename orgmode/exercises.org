#+TITLE: Every exercise in this book
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

Each chapter carries one or two, placed where the chapter has just taught
the thing they test. This page lists them all, in reading order. A taught
session can take a subset, and a reader working alone can find the one
they wanted.

Each has an id, and the id is stable across edits. So it is safe to refer
to one out loud, in a message, or in a shared document during a
workshop.
