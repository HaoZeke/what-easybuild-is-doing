Keypoints for each page are produced in reading order directly from the pages instead of manual maintenance.
The Carpentries refer to this as a digest ([[cite:wilsonSoftwareCarpentryLessons2016][Wilson 2016]]).
It has two purposes.
Before a page, it indicates whether the page's purpose is already known.
After finishing the book, it provides a page to reread instead of the entire book.
