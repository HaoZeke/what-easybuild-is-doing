#+TITLE: What to remember

#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil
Keypoints for each chapter are produced in reading order directly from the chapters rather than being manually maintained.
The Carpentries refer to this as a digest ([[cite:wilsonSoftwareCarpentryLessons2016][Wilson 2016]]).
It serves two purposes.
Before a chapter, it shows whether the chapter's purpose is already known.
After finishing the book, it provides a page to reread instead of the entire book.
