#+TITLE: A path through this book

#+OPTIONS: toc:nil num:nil

The chapters are numbered, and numbering is not a curriculum. This page is
the curriculum: levels sized at about an hour each, with the prerequisite
edges named, so you can start where your work starts.

The shape is Aalto's, from their *Hands-on Scientific Computing* map, and so
is the reasoning behind it: a reader arrives with a task rather than at
lesson one, and a map they can enter anywhere serves them better than a
sequence they have to abandon.

* Level A. Read a recipe

An hour. Nothing installed, nothing to configure.

| Read | For |
|---+---|

| [[file:02-a-toolchain-is-a-hierarchy.org][chapter 2]] | which dependencies a toolchain permits |

At the end you can read an easyconfig aloud and say what it will download,
which easyblock it will look for, and which dependencies it may use.
