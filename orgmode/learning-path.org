* The test
The official tutorial, the CSCS webinar, ExCALIBUR-tests and the EESSI suite each follow the same sequence.
The sequence consists of a test class, the pipeline, the configuration file, a performance number, and the portable suite.
There are two levels.
The grain matches the recipe.

Duration: two hours.
